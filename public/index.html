Methodology & disclaimer

Kalshi probabilities are bid–ask midpoints (last trade when the book is empty or uninformative). Polymarket probabilities are Yes prices per bucket. Each source's outcomes are normalized to sum to 100%. Data refreshes on demand and may be up to 15 minutes old — reload the page for fresh numbers.

These are market-implied probabilities, not forecasts and not financial advice. This site is not affiliated with the Bank of Canada, Kalshi, Polymarket, or BankofCanadaOdds.com.
